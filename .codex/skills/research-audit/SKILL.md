# Research audit

Use primary or authoritative sources when a fact may have changed, is specialized, or drives a consequential decision. Separate source facts from inferences. Record the conclusion, date, URLs/citations, scope, and limitation in the appropriate decision or experiment record. Do not treat search snippets, prior chat claims, or unstated integrations as proof.
